## Annex D: Relating days and hours to an operation

Days: **D Day** operation begins; **E Day** exercise begins; **M Day** mobilisation begins; **G Day / G Hour** decision to mount promulgated (6-figure DTG); **K Day** convoy system introduced on a lane.

Hours: **H Hour** operation/exercise starts (tactically, leading troops cross the start line); **A Hour, B Hour ...** later phases of a multi-phase operation (not using the reserved letters); **L Hour** first helicopter of heliborne assault wave lands (amphibious); **N Hour** first tactical nuclear detonation; **P Hour** first sticks of main force drop (airborne).

Numbering: `D-2, D-1, D, D+1, D+2`. Hours/minutes: `H - 1 hour`, `H - 30 minutes`, `H Hour`, `H + 1 hour` (words "hour(s)"/"minute(s)" always shown; "plus"/"minus" allowed). Operational writing: `H + 20 min`, `H + 3 hr`.

Nickname/code word goes before the letter: `BALD EAGLE - E DAY`, `ANVIL EXPRESS - H Hour`. Several simultaneous operations may use any other letter except those reserved above.

## Annex E: Units of measurement

Symbols never take a plural `s` (`10 lb`). No space or hyphen between prefix and unit (`cm`, `kl`, `kilogram`). Prefixes only with units marked *; never combine prefixes.

| Quantity | Unit → abbreviation (alternative) |
|---|---|
| Length | inch in ("), foot ft ('), yard yd, mile mile (M), fathom fath (fm), nautical mile nm, millimetre mm, centimetre cm, decimetre dm, *metre m, kilometre km |
| Area | sq in (in²), sq ft (ft²), sq yd (yd²), sq mile (mile²), *square metre sq m (m²) |
| Volume | cu in (in³), cubic foot cft (ft³), cu yd (yd³), cubic centimetre cc (cm³), *cubic metre cu m (m³) |
| Capacity | pint pt, quart qt, gallon gal, fluid ounce fl oz, *litre l, millilitre ml |
| Angle | second sec ("), minute min ('), degree deg (°), *radian rad, *steradian sr |
| Time | second sec, minute min, hour hr |
| Speed | ft/sec, mph, knot kt, m/sec, km/hr (kph), rpm (rev/min), rad/sec, velocity vel |
| Mass | ounce oz, pound lb, hundredweight cwt, long ton (2240 lb) ton, short ton ST, *gram g, kilogram kg, tonne t, maund md, kiloton KT |
| Density | g/cc (g/cm³), lb/cft (lb/ft³), t/cu m (t/m³) |
| Force | poundal pdl, lbf, ozf, tonf, kgf, dyne dyn, *newton N |
| Torque | lbf ft, Nm, dyn cm, kgf m |
| Work/energy | ft lbf, joule J, m kgf, *watt W, *kilowatt hour kWh |

| Heat | Btu, calorie cal, kilocalorie kcal |

| Electrical | *ampere amp, *volt V, *ohm Ω, *henry H, *farad F, *hertz Hz, cps |
| Other | decibel dB, röntgen R, *becquerel Bq, *gray Gy, *sievert Sv, coulomb C, tesla T, weber W, siemens S |

Prefixes: exa E (10^18), peta P, tera T, giga G, mega M, kilo k, hecto h, deca da, deci d, centi c, milli m, micro u, nano n, pico p, femto f, atto a (10^-18).

To foreign forces: their customary units first, ours in brackets, e.g. `60.32 mm +1.59/-1.58 (2 3/8 + 1/16 in)`. No equivalents needed where a recognised standard unit exists. First use in full (originating country's term, e.g. `imperial gallon (imp gal)`), then abbreviated.
