# JSSDM 2022 Section 3: Service Correspondence (paras 0301-0322, Annexes A-J)

Verbatim text: `section3-raw.txt`. Printed examples: `../pages/3B-1.png` ... `3J-4.png`. All Section 2 rules still apply unless a rule here overrides them.

## General (0301-0308)

- Standard layouts by purpose. **Serif font (e.g. Times New Roman) preferred** for formal correspondence; font size per commander/staff preference (0301).
- Text = **introduction** (purpose + background) → **theme** (case, arguments, problems in logical order) → **conclusion** (sums up; leaves reader in no doubt of the writer's orders, wishes, opinions or recommendations). Headings for these are seldom used, but the transitions must be obvious (0302).
- **Third person** normally. First person allowed in: formal letters; DO letters; a commander's personal views/praise/blame signed personally; HQ/department letters ("we"). May open and close in first person and use third person in between (0303).
- Printed letterhead: typescript below still follows the standard layout (0304).
- Address (0305): to other Services, government or civilians give full postal address of originator and all recipients, postcode as last line. Service practice uses recognised abbreviations. To an individual: name/appointment first, then branch/directorate, then HQ/unit, then postal address.
- MOD-originated correspondence follows MOD rules (0306).
- Salutation and ending depend on relative rank and relationship (0307; Annex A).
- Variations (0308): internal correspondence omits postal address; many action addressees may go below the text or on a separate sheet, or write `Distribution List <no/letter>` in place of the first action addressee.
- **Condolence letters**: written as private letters; no official headed paper; salutation and ending handwritten; **paragraphs not numbered**; no file number; no headings; addressee's name and address omitted.

## The seven types (0309)

Commanded, directed, routine, formal, demi-official (DO), signal messages (Section 15), internal correspondence (memorandum, loose minute, note sheet). **No abbreviations in commanded, formal and DO letters** except decorations, qualifications and arms/services.

| Type | When / who | Key format points | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Commanded** (0310) | Major policy and directives of the Service Chiefs and above; signed at PSO level | Not bound by normal rules. Opens "I am commanded by ... to inform you that ..."; then "I am to say that ..."; full words, no abbreviations; full-word rank/appointment in signature block; `To:` list below | 3B-1 |

| **Routine** (0312-0314) | Normal correspondence within and between Services, also individuals on personal matters | Full Section 2 layout: classification, precedence, abbreviated address, Tel/Email, reference opposite date (`Sep 22`), subject heading, `Refs:` lettered, numbered paras, maximum abbreviation, signature `For CGS`, `Distr:` → `Extl:` `Act:` / `Info:` → `Internal:` `Act:` / `Info:` | 3D-1, 3D-2 |
| **Routine to civilians** (0313) | Civil authorities/public | Routine layout **with salutation and conventional ending**; previous correspondence referred to in the text; **no Service terminology or abbreviations** (see 0222) | - |
| **Formal** (0315-0317) | Ceremonial matters and formal submissions; **only to seniors or equals**; signed by originator. | **Memorandum** (0319) | Quick routine matters between departments/directorates/branches; usually manuscript; rarely filed | `MEMORANDUM` heading; `From:` / `To:`; `Tel:` / `Dt:`; `Our ref:` / `Your ref:`; `Subj:`; numbered paras; signed with name only. Completed in manuscript unless special reason | 3G-1 |

## Note sheet rules (Annex J notes)

1. Wide margin printed/ruled on both sides; narrower right margin titled `Encl No` (underlined, level with the file reference).
2. Same classification as the file.
3. File reference typed from the left margin of the first page; reference and subject heading may be repeated on every page.
4. **Note numbers in Arabic numerals, underlined**, centred above each note.
5. Enclosures numbered by the note that docketed them: first enclosure for Note 1 = `1A`; its attachments `1B`, `1C`... An enclosure docketed under Note 7 is `7A` even if it is the fourth enclosure.
6. A single paragraph in a note is not numbered; two or more are numbered.
7. Subscription: name, rank, appointment, then telephone and date (a stamp may replace the typed block).
8. The next addressee's appointment is written at the left margin under the note; each addressee **crosses out his appointment** after endorsing and signing. Two or more addressees: one below the other, two spaces apart, in the order they must see it.
9. Every incoming and outgoing paper in the file gets an enclosure number and is docketed under the related note. Several replies: separate notes, separate enclosure numbers.
10. Typical noting language: `Ref Encl 1A and 2A.`, `Ref Note 3.`, `Notes 1-4 ante.`, `Your concurrence is req.`, `Agreed. Info HQ 10 Div accordingly.`

## Email (0322)

- Official email must look and read formally; choose a professional format.
- Routine issues may go by email; commanded/directed/routine/formal/DO letters keep their place and may be **attached** if permissible.
- Email can be hacked and the provider keeps a copy: take care with sensitive material; **never send classified military information by email unless the system is secure**.
- Salutation and complimentary ending are the norm.

## Annex A: Styles of address

- **DO letters**: after the surname show the abbreviated Service/corps/regiment/branch for Army officers up to Lt Col, Navy up to Commodore, **all** Air Force officers.
- Salutations for `Lieutenant Colonel A N Amin Ahmed, EME`, most to least formal: `Dear Colonel Ahmed` → `Dear Colonel` → `Dear Amin` → `My dear Amin`. An equal/senior who does not know him: `Dear Ahmed`. Use generic rank: Maj Gen/Brig Gen → `Dear General`; Lt Col → `Dear Colonel`.
- **Civilians**: `Dear Sir`/`Dear Madam` or `Dear (Mr) Rafiq` + `Yours faithfully`. Corporate body (city council, society): `Gentlemen` + `Yours faithfully`. Firm when formality not needed: `Dear Sirs` + `Yours faithfully`. High commissioners and ambassadors: ending `Yours faithfully`.
- Salutation/ending inserted in manuscript by the signatory, except formal letters (typed).
- Decoration/qualification symbols (BU, BB, SBP, ndc, afwc, psc, G+, te...) used in official and DO letters, invitation cards, records, Service lists, **not in the text** of a letter (as with minutes of meetings).

### Army (Appx 1)
- MOD: `Ministry of Defence (D-II)` / `Sher-E-Bangla Nagar` / `Ganabhaban Complex` / `Dhaka`; if branch unknown use `Procurement Section`.
- Formations: policy matters to the **commander**, other matters to the **HQ**; units: policy to the CO/OC, routine to the unit. Signal address = formation/unit title only. `HQ` = the formation HQ generally.
- Examples (full → abbreviated): The Chief of Army Staff, Bangladesh Army → `CAS BD Army`; Army Headquarters, General Staff Branch, Staff Duties Directorate → `AHQ GS Br, SD Dte`; operational `AHQ GS Br (MO Dte)`. GOC 9 Infantry Division → `GOC 9 Inf Div`; `HQ 9 Inf Div`; `Main/Rear HQ 9 Inf Div`. `Comd 87 Inf Bde`; `HQ 87 Inf Bde`. `CO 1 EB`; `1 BIR`; `19 EB (Div Sp)`. `OC 88 Fd Bty`; `88 Fd Bty`.
- Major unit (regiment, battalion) commander = **Commanding Officer**; minor unit (battery, company) = **Officer Commanding**.

### Navy (Appx 2)
- MOD: `Ministry of Defence (D-6)`, else `Procurement Section`.
- **Always address the appointment holder**: CNS, COMCHIT, COMBAN, CSD, COMDHAKA, COMFLOT WEST, COMNAV, COMSWADS, COMSUB, SNO (A), COMKHUL, COMPATRON 41, `CO BNS ISSA KHAN`, `CO NSD`, `OIC LCT-103`.
- Ships/establishments: `The Commanding Officer BNS SHADHINOTA`. Ship names in CAPITALS, **never in quotation marks, never without prefix** (BNS, BNFD SUNDARBAN, BNT, BNFC; same for foreign ships with their prefixes).
- Personal letters to CNS only when his personal attention is needed: `The Chief of Naval Staff` / `Naval Headquarters` / `Banani` / `Dhaka 1213`.
- To NHQ: `Naval Headquarters` / `<Branch>` / `Directorate of <...>` / `Banani 1213`. From NHQ: routine letter form, e.g. `Naval Headquarters` / `Operations Branch` / `Directorate of Naval Operations` / `Banani 1213` / `Dhaka`.

### Air Force (Appx 3)
- MOD: `Ministry of Defence (D-7)`, else `Law Cell`.
- Policy and formal correspondence to the **appointment**; routine correspondence, orders and instructions to the **formation/unit**. Include the action branch abbreviation when known.
- Examples: `OC/AOC BAF BSR` (bases BBD, CXB, MTR, PKP, ZHR); `ADOC Dhaka`; `Air HQ` / `Dte Air Trg` / `Dhaka Cantt`; `OC PDC Dhaka`; `OC 3 Sqn`; `OC 1 FU`.
